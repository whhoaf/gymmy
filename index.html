Gymmy

Gymmy is a personal training coach with a single user: its owner. It runs on the owner's private server and talks to the owner in a private Discord server.

With the owner's permission, Gymmy reads the owner's own Google Health data (sleep, heart rate and heart rate variability, activity and workouts, body measurements) to plan and adjust training sessions.

Gymmy is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.
