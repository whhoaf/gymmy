Gymmy privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Who uses Gymmy

Gymmy is a private app with one user, its owner. It is not available to anyone else.

What Gymmy reads

After the owner signs in with Google and gives consent, Gymmy reads the owner's Google Health data: sleep, heart rate and heart rate variability, activity, workouts and body measurements. Access is read-only. Gymmy never writes to or deletes anything in the owner's Google account.

How the data is used

Only to plan, adjust and review the owner's own training. The data is sent to Gymmy's AI model provider only to generate the owner's coaching replies. It is not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used by Gymmy to train AI models.

Where it is kept

On the owner's private server, in files only the owner's account can read.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Gymmy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke Gymmy's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and can delete the stored data from the server.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/whhoaf/gymmy.
